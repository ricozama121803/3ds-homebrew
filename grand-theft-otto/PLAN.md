# Grand Theft Otto - plan

Top-down open-world crime sandbox for the 3DS, in an LA-inspired city. Player: a human criminal in a prison-orange jumpsuit.
Art: pixel-art sprites generated by script. First version: sandbox (cash, cops, chaos); missions come later.

## Decisions so far
- Player: a human criminal (orange jumpsuit, black beanie, loot bag on his back). Changed from Tuffy the elephant after Stage 1.
  NPCs (pedestrians, cops, drivers) are humans too.
- Violence: cartoon, but with blood, and the blood is RED. Hits spray red splatters and leave stains on the ground
  that fade after a while. Applies to everyone: the player, pedestrians, cops. Downed pedestrians still drop cash.
- City: LA-inspired (changed from Fullerton after Stage 1), stylised and squeezed to fit 160x160 tiles: ocean + Santa Monica pier + Venice beach on the west,
  Hollywood + the Hollywood sign + Griffith Observatory + hills to the north, Beverly Hills, downtown skyscrapers, Dodger Stadium, the concrete LA River and
  the 5 on the east, the 405 / 10 / 101 freeways, LAX in the south. Skyscrapers are 'tall' buildings (extra 3D pop).
- Art: sprites generated by Python (PIL), 8-16 rotation frames or runtime rotation, into a texture atlas, same tex3ds -> bin2s pipeline as Tuffy Run. Rotating sprites for cars/people.
- Tech: C + citro2d, game logic separate from drawing (so a PC preview + bot tests work), 16 px tiles, 160 x 160 tile world.

## Controls (original 3DS has one stick)
- Circle Pad: move and aim. Auto-aim locks the nearest target in front. New 3DS C-stick optional later.
- A: shoot / punch. B: enter or exit a car. X / Y: next / previous weapon. L: handbrake. R: horn/siren toggle in cop car.
- Touch (bottom screen): tap a weapon icon to switch, tap the minimap to zoom.
- Driving: Circle Pad left/right steers, A gas, B brake/reverse (B also exits when stopped), L handbrake.

## Systems
- Life: 100 health + 100 armor. Getting hit sprays red. Pickups: health, armor, weapons/ammo, cash. 0 HP = WASTED: respawn at hospital, lose ~10% cash and weapons.
  Arrested (cop touches you at 1+ stars while on foot and slow) = BUSTED: lose weapons and ~20% cash, respawn at police station.
- Cars: arcade physics (accel, steering, lateral grip, handbrake drift), tile + car collisions, car health (smoke at 30%, fire at 10%, explodes at 0).
  Classes: compact, sedan, sports, van, taxi, police, SWAT truck. Carjack drivers in traffic; parked cars are free.
- Weapons: fists, pistol, SMG, shotgun (later: bat, rocket). Ammo counts. Bullets have travel time and hit pedestrians, cops, cars.
- Pedestrians: wander sidewalks, flee gunfire, drop cash when downed (red splatter on the ground). Traffic follows the road grid, stops at crashes, honks.
- Wanted level (1-5 stars):
  - 1: hit a pedestrian / steal a car in view of a cop. Cops on foot.
  - 2: shoot a gun in public. Cop cars.
  - 3: shoot a cop. More cars, roadblocks.
  - 4: SWAT trucks. 5: SWAT + helicopter searchlight (stretch).
  - Heat cools down after N seconds out of sight; Pay 'n' Spray garage resets it (and repaints the car).
- HUD: top screen = game + floating health above player when hit. Bottom = minimap (roads, cops as red/blue dots, missions later), life/armor bars, cash, stars, weapon + ammo.
- 3D: buildings, trees and cars get parallax depth; roofs "pop out" when the 3D slider is up.
- Save: cash, best wanted survival time, unlocked weapons -> sdmc:/3ds/grandtitanauto.sav.

## World layout (LA-inspired; user to correct/add)
- West: Pacific Ocean, beach, Santa Monica Pier with a Ferris wheel, Venice Beach boardwalk + canals.
- North: Hollywood Hills with the Hollywood sign, Griffith Park and the Observatory; Hollywood Blvd with the Walk of Fame and the Chinese Theatre;
  Sunset Strip; Beverly Hills (palm-lined streets, big houses with pools, Rodeo Drive).
- Centre / east: downtown skyscrapers, Union Station, an arena, City Hall; Dodger Stadium to the north-east; the concrete LA River and the 5 freeway on the east edge.
- Freeways: 405 (north-south, west side), 10 (east-west, south of downtown), 101 (east-west, north of downtown). Streets bridge them.
- South: Koreatown / mid-city apartments and strip malls, Exposition Park with the Coliseum, LAX with runways and the Theme Building.

## Performance budget (original 3DS is the target to protect)
- Live entities: cars <= 14, pedestrians <= 24, cops <= 8, bullets <= 32, particles <= 64. Spawn/despawn by distance from the camera.
- Cop pathing: BFS flow field over a limited radius, recomputed a few times per second, spread across frames.
- Tiles drawn from one atlas; only the tiles on screen. Target 60 fps with 3D on; fixed-step physics so speed is right even at 30 fps.

## Milestones (each one is an installable CIA)
1. Map, scrolling camera, walking, tile collision. Test: walk everywhere, no getting stuck; preview frames.
2. Cars: driving, physics, enter/exit, carjack, crash damage/explosion. Test: bot drives lap; collision fuzz test.
3. Shooting, weapons, life/armor, pickups, WASTED. Test: damage math, ammo, respawn.
4. Pedestrians and traffic AI. Test: 10-minute headless run: no stuck cars, no entity leaks, stays under budget.
5. Cops and wanted levels, BUSTED, Pay 'n' Spray. Test: cops always reach the player; 5-star chase is survivable; cooldown works.
6. HUD, minimap, 3D depth, sound-free polish, title screen, icon/banner. Test: preview frames, triangle budget.
7. Cash economy, saving, first missions (taxi, delivery, hit list).
