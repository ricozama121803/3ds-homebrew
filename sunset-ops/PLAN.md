# Sunset Ops - plan

First-person team deathmatch for the 3DS, in the style of a console shooter (HUD, red-dot sights, muzzle flash, radar, kill feed) on a small
Nuketown-style desert cul-de-sac map. 4 vs 4: you and three bots against four bots. First team to 30 kills, or the higher score after 8 minutes.

## Controls (as requested)
- X / B / Y / A: move forward / back / strafe left / strafe right (the face buttons are laid out like a d-pad)
- Circle Pad (left stick): look around (the New 3DS C-stick adds to it)
- R: shoot (ZR too). L: aim down sights (ZL too) - zooms, centres the sight and slows the look speed while aiming
- D-pad left/right: previous / next weapon. D-pad up: grenade. D-pad down: heal (uses a medkit)
- SELECT or a tap on the touch screen: reload (an empty magazine also reloads by itself). START: pause (look speed + invert Y live there)

## Decisions
- Single C code base, 3D only (no 2D fallback: a first-person game can't have one). Same citro3d approach as Grand Theft Otto: the CPU builds
  triangle lists with all lighting baked into vertex colours, a pass-through vertex shader, one texture per material. If the GPU setup fails the
  game shows a message on the bottom screen instead of crashing silently.
- Map: axis-aligned boxes only (`source/map.c`). Static geometry is built once into one vertex buffer sorted by texture; the ground is lit with a
  baked low-sun shadow map. Soldiers, effects, pickups and the first-person gun are rebuilt every frame (fixed capacities, checked by `host/stress.c`).
- Gun: drawn in view space with its own projection and its own slice of the depth range (so it never clips into walls). Aiming down sights moves it
  to the centre and zooms the world only, not the gun.
- Bots navigate with a per-nav-point distance field on a 1 m grid (`navd`), shoot hitscan with an aim error that grows with range, take cover
  by strafing, throw grenades, and spot you when you fire. Their damage is lower than the player's.
- Health does not regenerate: heal with D-pad down (3 medkits max, pickups on the map). Spawn protection for 2 s.
- Textures are generated by `assets/make_textures.py` (no photos); tex3ds turns them into mip-mapped .t3x files that `assets/pack_textures.py` packs into one blob.
- Settings (look speed, invert Y) and best kills are saved to `sdmc:/3ds/sunsetops.sav`.

## Tests (no console needed)
- `host/simtest.c`: map and navigation sanity (spawns/pickups free and connected), then bot-vs-bot matches (no one stuck, no one in a wall, teams balanced).
- `host/playtest.c`: scripted checks of every control (movement mapping, aiming slows the look, weapons, reload, heal, grenade, kills, respawn, pause, match end).
- `host/stress.c`: random button mashing for several matches; checks for NaNs, wall clipping, vertex-group overflow.
- `host/preview.sh`: software-rasterised frames of the same triangles plus the HUD (`host/c2d_shim.h` stands in for citro2d).

## Ideas for later
Sound effects, more maps, a sniper rifle with a scope overlay, killstreaks, local wireless matches.

## Graphics (updated)
- Models are smooth ellipsoids / tapered cylinders / rounded boxes (`tess_*` in `scene.c`). Soldiers and guns are tessellated once into model-space caches
  (position, normal, base colour with camo and height shading baked in) and only transformed + lit per frame, so extra detail costs the GPU, not the CPU.
- Soldiers have three detail levels (belts, pouches, plates, goggles, ears, fingers only for the two nearest, and only at High quality).
- High quality adds map props (street lamps, window sills, roof units, benches, planters, car lights and mirrors). Low skips them.
- Budgets (checked by `host/stress.c`): dynamic vertex groups have fixed capacities; static world is about 18.6k triangles at Low and 25.6k at High.
- Safety: the pause menu has Graphics LOW/HIGH (R, applies after restart) and an FPS counter (L). A lock file `sdmc:/3ds/sunsetops.lock` exists for the first 30 s of a launch;
  if the game dies in that window the next launch starts in Low and says so.

## Haze, baked shadows, painted textures
- Haze: the vertex shader writes a distance amount into the colour alpha and texture-environment stage 1 interpolates towards a warm haze colour (22 m to 190 m);
  only opaque world and soldier passes use it. The sun veil (warm wash when looking into the sun) is drawn by the HUD. Pause menu can turn the haze off.
- Baked shadows: ground (soldier-free, 1 m lattice) now also gets shadows from tree canopies; tall sun-facing walls are shaded on a 1.5 m grid (neighbour shadows plus the shadow
  under the roof overhang); thin props (window panes, sills) sample the sun once.
- Textures: `assets/make_textures.py` paints a 256x256 character atlas (camo for both teams, webbing, face, leather, glove, scratched metal, polymer, bronze, wood, scarves).
  Regions are listed in `TXR` in scene.c. Soldier and gun parts carry UVs from their primitive (sphere, cylinder, rounded box) and are captured into the model caches with them.
